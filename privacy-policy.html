PRIVACY POLICY

At Body Tee, we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store,
and protect your personal information when you visit our website or place an order.

Last Updated: October 15, 2026

01. Information We Collect

When you place an order or contact us, we may collect the following information, depending on the information you provide and the features available on our website:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and delivery address.
  • Order Information: Selected T-shirt, size, quantity, and special instructions.
  • Payment Information: Details necessary to confirm payments through the agreed payment method. Our website does not currently have an integrated online payment gateway.
  • Technical Information: IP address, browser type, and device information that may be processed by our hosting provider or related services.

02. How We Collect Your Information

We may collect information when you place an order, send us an email, or contact us with inquiries. Clicking the “Order Now” button may transfer your order details to your email application for review and submission.

Our hosting provider or related services may also process technical information to maintain website functionality and security.

03. Why We Collect Your Information

We may process personal information to:

  • Receive and fulfill T-shirt orders.
  • Communicate with customers and respond to inquiries.
  • Coordinate deliveries and confirm payments.
  • Maintain website functionality and security.
  • Keep necessary business records and comply with legal obligations.

All processing must have an appropriate lawful basis under applicable law.

04. How We Use Your Information

We use your information provided to process orders, confirm product selections, coordinate deliveries, verify payments when applicable, and address customer concerns. We will not use personal information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate lawful basis.

05. Who We Share Your Information With

Body Tee does not sell customers’ personal information. When necessary, we may share relevant information with:

  • Email Providers: To send and receive order requests and communications.
  • Hosting Providers: To operate, maintain, and secure the website.
  • Delivery Services: To deliver orders using the necessary customer details.
  • Payment Providers: To facilitate or confirm payments, when applicable.
  • Legal Authorities: When disclosure is required by law or a valid legal request.

We will limit information sharing to what is necessary for the relevant purpose, subject to applicable law.

06. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support basic functionality and other features, depending on its configuration. The actual cookie practices have not yet been verified for this prototype.

Analytics and advertising tracking tools have not been confirmed as part of the current setup. This section will be updated if such technologies are implemented.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings, although disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

07. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill orders, respond to inquiries, maintain required records, and comply with legal obligations. When information is no longer needed, we will take reasonable steps to securely delete or anonymize it where appropriate and permitted by law.

08. Data Security

We aim to implement reasonable organizational, physical, and technical measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. However, no electronic transmission or storage method is completely secure.

09. Your Privacy Rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 and other applicable laws, you may have the right to:

  • Be Informed: Know how your personal information is collected, used, stored, and processed.
  • Access: Request access to your personal information held by us.
  • Rectification: Request corrections to inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Erasure or Blocking: Request the removal or blocking of personal information when the conditions under applicable law are met.
  • Object: Object to certain processing activities when permitted by law.
  • Data Portability: Request a copy of qualifying personal information in a structured, commonly used, and portable format, subject to applicable conditions.
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent when processing is based on consent, subject to applicable legal limitations.
  • File a Complaint: Lodge a complaint with the appropriate authority if you believe your privacy rights have been violated.
  • Seek Appropriate Remedies: Pursue applicable remedies under the law when your rights have been infringed.

These rights are subject to applicable legal conditions and limitations. Contact us through the details below to exercise your rights. Reasonable identity verification may be required.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for general customers and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate authorization where required by law. We will take reasonable steps to address inappropriate collection in accordance with applicable requirements.

11. International Data Transfers

Some of the service providers used by Body Tee may process personal information through systems located outside the Philippines.

The location of data processing will depend on the services and platforms actually used. Where personal information is transferred internationally, we will take appropriate steps to comply with applicable data protection requirements.

This section will be reviewed once our website hosting provider, email service, and other relevant service providers have been identified.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, or data-handling practices change. Revised versions will be published on this page with an updated date. We encourage customers to review this policy periodically.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your personal information, you may contact Body Tee through our Contact Us page.

We will respond to privacy inquiries in accordance with applicable law.
